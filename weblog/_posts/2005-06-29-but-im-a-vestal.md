The first episode of [Empire](http://abc.go.com/movies/empire.html), ABC's new [miniseries](http://www.newyorker.com/critics/television/) about Rome during the time of [Augustus](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caesar_Augustus), was silly, lurid, and chock-full of ridiculous inaccuracies.

I think the series is off to a damn fine start.

It's a gorgeous spectacle, and the actors are immensely pleasurable to watch, especially the (OK, admittedly on-target) depictions of Brutus (spineless), Cassius (slimy), and Antony (brutal). They've definitely got me hooked for the rest of the series.

If you're following it, the middle hundred pages or so of H. H. Scullard's *[From the Gracchi to Nero](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0415025273?v=glance)* are an accessible -- and absolutely peerless -- crib sheet for the times.
