Derek's post about [berbere on injera](http://www.earthwidemoth.com/mt/archives/000955.html) got me homesick for some of DC's Ethiopian restaurants. Which got me thinking that I'm really, really going to enjoy being at MLA in December, just for the food, although I'm sure I'll be stressed from interviewing. First on the list of priorities is [Dukem](http://www.dukemrestaurant.com/) for a proper Ethiopian dinner, but I'm also thinking Eat First on H Street in Chinatown for the best (and cheap!) Cantonese roast duck I've ever had, taking the Metro up to [Negril](http://www.washingtonpost.com/ac2/wp-dyn?node=entertainment/profile&id=794292) in Silver Spring for a fine Jamaican lunch, [Mama Ayesha's](http://www.washingtonpost.com/ac2/wp-dyn?node=entertainment/profile&id=792220&typeId=2) in Adams Morgan for Lebanese, maybe [Jaleo](http://www.jaleo.com/downtown.html) for tapas (I used to temp just up the street) after the theater rush, definitely [Half Moon BBQ](http://www.halfmoonbbq.com/c/bbq/main.html) with live music in Silver Spring, and -- though it's a little more than walking distance from the Silver Spring Metro stop; like a five-minute cab ride down Wayne Avenue -- El Gavilan's Salvadorean food is not to be missed.

Dang I'm hungry.
