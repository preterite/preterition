(Warning for grownups: this is, by insinuation, a deeply crude post.)

I thought it was initially unremarkable: nothing more than [the paper of record](http://www.nytimes.com/pages/index.html) doing a little editorial slobbering over the way [those two adorable NYU moppets](http://www.olsentwins.net/) were embarassedly pretending (well, not really: of course they'd never be so distasteful) [to be poor](http://www.nytimes.com/2005/03/06/fashion/06olsen.html). I mentioned it to the Tutor, who [has his own fine](http://www.thehappytutor.com/archives/2005/03/dumpster_chic_f.html) take on what's going on. But then I saw an English professor [who talks about herself in the third person](http://margaretsoltan.phenominet.com/) reference Paul Fussell's rather obnoxious work on class in relation to that story, and y'know, [she's pretty funny](http://margaretsoltan.phenominet.com/2005/03/snapshots-from-home-pauvre-although.html), until she gets to the declaration that "Americans have wealth."

It's really a shame that the English departments and the sociology departments don't talk much these days. They used to be so close.

But what Margaret Soltan did for me -- yes, I know it's a betrayal, dear liberal reader -- was to set me on a search that ended at the doorstep of [the Volokh conspirators](http://volokh.com/archives/archive_2005_02_27-2005_03_05.shtml#1110056255). While the Olsen twins fashion story might serve as one possible primary argument, here's the supporting evidence that the *New York Times* is a [fluffer](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluffer) for wealth and privilege: gosh, [making six figures sure is tough](http://www.nytimes.com/2005/02/27/fashion/27200K.html?ex=1267160400&en=f78968e3bebbd60f&ei=5090), and [trying to find an apartment on a six-figure budget is even worse](http://www.nytimes.com/2005/03/06/realestate/06cov.html?8dpc)!

Please, somebody fetch The Gray Lady a warm washcloth. She's fixin to head out for the Hamptons, but she's got [wealth bondage](http://www.thehappytutor.com/) all over her face.
