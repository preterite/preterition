This isn't a review of the excellent [all-day session](http://joe.english.purdue.edu/su05/drupalworkshop/) [on](http://bleckblog.org/?q=node/22) [using](http://cyberdash.com/node/383) [Drupal](http://drupal.org/) as a writing course [community content management system](http://kairosnews.org/node/4313), but just a way to say thanks to [Charlie Lowe](http://cyberdash.com/), [Jim Kalmbach](http://www.hu.mtu.edu/~rselfe/520/patty_1.htm), [Bradley Bleck](http://bleckblog.org/), and [Tim Lindgren](http://www.timlindgren.com/) (as well as some helpful long-distance commentary from [Samantha Blackmon](http://joe.english.purdue.edu/blog/) and [Clancy Ratliff](http://www.culturecat.net/)) for a great workshop. [Kieran Lal](http://civicspacelabs.org/home/blog/kieran) assisted as well with some tutoring and a short and impressive presentation on [CivicSpace](http://civicspacelabs.org/home/civicspace), the reincarnation (?) of the DeanSpace distributed open source content management system designed specifically for the needs of a grassroots political campaign. I learned lots and lots and lots today -- my brain hurts -- and I'm looking forward to trying out some of Drupal's way-beyond-just-blogging capabilities with my students in the fall.

My presentation's tomorrow morning. (And, uh, I could still really use some [help with a title](/weblog/archives/2005/06/16/entitlement/).)
