Warren Zevon died a little over 16 years ago, and while he's mostly known today for his AOR hits (*"Knock knock." "Who's there?" "Ah." "Ah who?"*), his deeper cuts ("Desperadoes Under the Eaves," "The Hula Hula Boys," "If You Won't Leave Me I'll Find Somebody Who Will") are among my all-time favorite songs. There were a [couple bits](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/10/26/business/michael-milken-trump-opportunity-zones.html) of [recent news](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/company-with-ties-to-trumps-brother-robert-awarded-33-million-government-contract/2019/10/26/f67164d2-f688-11e9-829d-87b12c2f85dd_story.html) that made me [cue up the playlist](https://music.apple.com/us/playlist/warren-zevon/pl.u-mJXJEuN76Ab).
