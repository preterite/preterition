Saturday night, twenty-two of us went to see the Franco Zeffirelli [production](http://query.nytimes.com/gst/fullpage.html?res=9C06E0D61339F935A1575AC0A9649C8B63&sec=&spon=&partner=permalink&exprod=permalink) of [*Turandot*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turandot) at the Metropolitan Opera (click for a larger version):

[![the group, in uniform](/weblog/assets/met_cadets_small.jpg)](/weblog/assets/met_cadets_large.jpg)

At intermission, we were taken backstage and allowed to watch the mechanics and massive coordination involved in changing Zeffirelli's glorious sets, and met and talked with the charming soprano [Erika Sunnegårdh](http://www.metoperafamily.org/metopera/season/production.aspx?id=8827&detect=yes), who sang in the title role.

It was a pretty good Saturday night.
