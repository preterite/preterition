I just got a big shock: turned on the tube, and there's Bob Costas in a broadcast TV commercial, declaring that "Writing is everybody's business." I know [who's behind this spot](http://www.writingcommission.org/), and I'm glad to see some [familiar names](http://www.writingcommission.org/members.html) -- but I'm not sure what's going on with the intersection with the College Board ("education, business, and policy-making communities"?), and while one part of me is happy that some of our scholars are reclaiming the national public discourse on writing, another part of me asks: who's bankrolling you, Bob Costas, and what are they after?

I'm sorry if I'm behind the curve on this: does anybody else have more info? Is this [John's hoped-for re-branding](http://faculty.deanza.fhda.edu/jocalo/2005/03/19), a turf grab serving [conservative](http://www.whitehouse.gov/news/reports/no-child-left-behind.html) (pardon my amazement at a White House press release that first shifts the blame for educational failures onto the "self-doubt" of the "uneducated" and then immediately makes multiple obvious surface-level correctness errors) and commercial [standardized-testing](http://www.collegeboard.com/) interests, both, neither?
