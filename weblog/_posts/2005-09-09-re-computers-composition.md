Some good, smart people have [already](http://kairosnews.org/node/4243) [noted](http://ccccip.org/node/125) the troubling nature of a certain [journal](http://www.bgsu.edu/cconline/) publisher's attitude towards intellectual property, as well as the troubling nature of said publisher's [business practices](http://cavlec.yarinareth.net/archives/2004/01/12/so-whats-this-elsevier-thing/). It's rather more difficult to describe as merely "troubling" the fact that said publisher is <del>involved in</del> [connected](http://crookedtimber.org/2005/09/09/books-and-bombs/) (via parent company Reed Elsevier; see comments below) to the [international arms trade](http://www.idiolect.org.uk/notes/archives/politics/academics_and_elsevi.html).

It might be worth thinking about the ten million [land mines](http://www.unicef.org/graca/mines.htm) and eight thousand amputee children in Angola before submitting that article to *Computers and Composition*.
