Lauralea and I went for a short, hilly run this morning -- net change in elevation of 350 feet, repeated several times over 2.5 miles -- and then went to [Lost River](http://www.lostriversp.com/) [State Park](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_River_State_Park) for a hike up Big Ridge (elevation 3200-3300 feet) on White Oak Trail. On the way, we passed groves of trees that looked almost exactly the way I imagine the edges of (nerd alert) [Lothlórien](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) looking.
