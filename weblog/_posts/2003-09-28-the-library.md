Rainy, rainy day. Still, this afternoon, I had the pleasure of hearing former Poet Laureate [Richard Wilbur](http://www.smith.edu/poetrycenter/bios.php?name=rwilbur) read some of his [poems](http://www.sewanee.edu/theology/str/strpoems.html#Wilbur) at our wonderful small [local](/weblog/assets/interior.jpg) [library's](/weblog/assets/knowledge.jpg) re-opening.

To help finance the renovation, the library sold bricks for its small [back](/weblog/assets/courtyard.jpg) [courtyard](/weblog/assets/courtyard2.jpg). I took a picture of [one](/weblog/assets/irvine_brick.jpg).

The reading was great. The library's beautiful. It was a good day.
