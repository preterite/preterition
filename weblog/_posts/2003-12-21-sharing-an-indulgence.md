A confession of weakness: in my flurry of Christmas shopping for father and brother and relations and friends, I'm afraid I took that guilty pleasure and got a CD for me, too. Yes, I'm weak and self-indulgent, so weak and self-indulgent as to play that same CD while I was wrapping presents.

And it's the best CD I've bought in years.

I'm sure some people are familiar with the wonderful singing of Washington, DC songbird [Eva](http://www.washingtonian.com/people/evacassidy.html) [Cassidy](http://www.oaksite.co.uk/bioblue.html); I actually came at her work from the other end, being a longtime fan of [Chuck Brown](http://www.wamu.org/mc/gogo.html), and not having known that she did the backing vocals on E.U.'s first album all by herself. But I gotta say: if you like jazz vocals at all (actually, I think the collaboration kinda walks between jazz, pop ballads, and blue-eyed soul), go out right now and find [this album](http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-srv/style/music/features/1browncassidy0309.htm). It's so, so good.

Go find some Chuck Brown, too, of course, especially his live albums (and if you enjoy them, there's also the much more high-energy -- to the point of being practically incendiary -- [Trouble](http://www.artistdirect.com/store/artist/album/0,,372774,00.html) [Funk](http://www.gyrofrog.com/tfunk.html)), and Eva Cassidy's other stuff is fine, fine, fine -- but ["The Other Side"](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/B000005FN2/qid=1071982999/sr=1-8/ref=sr_1_8/102-2905694-1005713?v=glance&s=music) is the best collection of soulful, blues-tinged jazz ballads and duets you'll hear in a long, long time.

And if Eva's "Over the Rainbow" doesn't bring a tear to your eye, there's something wrong with you.
