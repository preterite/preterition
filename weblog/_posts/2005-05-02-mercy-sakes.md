Ten Four [Ten Four](http://www.apple.com/macosx/) Pigpen, looks like we got ourselves a (unsupported media format).

[Jenny's looking](http://workingblue.org/su/?p=32) for traveling songs. If somebody were skilled enough to splice that C. W. McCall intro into the early snare-drum loading-up scene from *Aliens* that gets every military detail so right and concludes with Hudson crowing, "We're on an express elevator to Hell -- *goin' down*!" into the extended version of Ministry's "Jesus Built My Hotrod" and from there into Iggy Pop's "Repo Man" -- man, those'd be some left-lane passing tunes.

I ain't a trucker no more, and I'm old enough that I usually stay to the center on the freeway these days. But today, I'd bought Mac OS X Ten Four and put it in my bag, and I heard C. W. McCall on the radio on the drive home, and I was on the mountain road and:

Well, I had to put the hammer down and open up them cylinders and feel old Isaac Newton's heavy abstract ancient hand push my guts backwards as I pegged it up to 100 through them turns.

Drive safe, Jenny, and don't cut off no truckers, unless you're a [long-haired friend of Jesus in a chartreuse microbus](http://www.oldielyrics.com/lyrics/c_w_mccall/convoy.html). And in your collegetown destination, the index-finger wave from the steering wheel is a fine thing.
