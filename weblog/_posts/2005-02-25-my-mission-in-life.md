I had dinner with a good friend tonight, and described my [Father-Ong-as-Army-Chaplain](/weblog/archives/2005/02/24/army-dream/) dream to her and my thoughts on its likely provenance. [Wayne Booth](/weblog/archives/2005/02/24/on-booth-1/) mentions Ong (83) as one of rhetoric's "Major Rescuers," and Ong's spirit is certainly present throughout the text in Booth's attention to [the importance of audience](http://jac.gsu.edu/jac/5.1/Articles/2.htm) in rhetoric. The other source, I think, was in some [disturbing video footage from Iraq](http://www.bhd93.com/ambush.mpg) I came across this week: it definitely left an impression.

I related this to my friend, and -- having seen the video footage, and knowing my abiding interest in the uses of rhetoric -- she was quiet for a moment. "So what this dream is telling you," she said, "is that, subconsciously, you've linked your life's pursuit to tipping over outhouses."

"Yup."

"And you're OK with that."

Yup.
