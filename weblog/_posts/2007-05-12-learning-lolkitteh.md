[The Ratliff](http://culturecat.net/) compels, and one obeys.

Well, OK: [Clancy wrote](/weblog/2007/05/01/lolcats.html#comment-73177), "Mike, you HAVE to do an [I Can Has Cheezburger?](http://icanhascheezburger.com/) image," but while I can read and parody [LOLKitteh](http://icanhascheezburger.com/2007/04/24/lol-kitteh-as-a-second-language-lksl-101-in-five-easy-steps/), I'm far from being a native or even adequate speaker.

I'm doing my best to learn LOLKitteh, certainly, particularly given its recent emergence as one of the [fundamental philosophical discourses of modernity](http://www.metafilter.com/60588/An-evil-illegible-language-beckons-you). My efforts, however, yield slight return. Tink and Zeugma regard me indulgently as I practice the tense-shifts and contractions, but when I attempt to engage them in LOLKitteh, they flee to the litterboxen.

As Clancy has demonstrated, though, LOLKitteh allows us to speak of that which other discourses and other interlocutors (our friend and colleague [Joanna Howard](http://giovannamaria.typepad.com/2_board_alley/2007/05/lolcats_friday_.html) comes immediately to mind) forbid. There is, for example, the practice of [interrogating so-called 'flavor' as sociocultural and affective construct](http://icanhascheezburger.com/tag/flavor/).
