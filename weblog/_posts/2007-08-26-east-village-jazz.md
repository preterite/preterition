Had a fine time in the City today, seeing the [Charlie Parker Jazz Festival](http://www.summerstage.org/index1.aspx?BD=19702) at Tompkins Square Park. [Chico Hamilton](http://www.myspace.com/chicohamilton) and his band were terrific. Later, however, we were disappointed to hear that [Abbey Lincoln](http://www.npr.org/programs/jazzprofiles/archive/lincoln.html) wouldn't be able to perform. . .

. . . although the surprise of [Cassandra Wilson](http://www.cnn.com/SPECIALS/2001/americasbest/pro.cwilson.html) showing up to sing in her stead kinda eased that sting a bit.
