[The Happy Tutor](http://www.thehappytutor.com/), bless him, has posted [a brilliant set of exercises](http://www.thehappytutor.com/mt/archives/2004/08/the_art_of_blog.html) for first-year composition courses. Absolutely required material for those of us who ask college freshmen to keep weblogs, or even to write for a public.

My syllabus is under immediate overhaul, Tutor. If I didn't know better, I'd accuse you of mashing together [Peter Elbow](http://writingprogram.hfa.umass.edu/student_resources/course_texts/otwb/introduction.asp) and [Victor Vitanza](http://www.uta.edu/english/V/rhetcomp.html). Many thanks.
