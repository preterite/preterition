*A note on method:* the interactive diagram took me about forty minutes to create with Claude. I already had the clean data in my research vault; Claude wrote the JavaScript and CSS and scripted the extraction from my BibTeX sources database through the blog posts to the Mermaid-generated SVG. The minimal human labor cost of adaptation came from prior choices I've made in tooling and organizing and indexing the vault. I'd suggest that with properly tended data, one might easily adapt the theories of delivery proposed by Ridolfo and DeVoss to broader inquiries concerning technologies of rhetorical practice and economic self-appropriation.
