Now I got something you have to see: I've made a copy of a copy of the dataset, and I've revised the [Robinson's Zoetrope](/weblog/2026/10/29/dataset-playback-agent-memory-part-2.html) tool. Like dressing up for [Halloween](/weblog/2026/10/26/agent-memory-trick-treat-receipt.html): you've met the machine underneath.

{% include zoe-dataset-player.html caption="`Figure 1`: *Zoe 2.1 (Dataset Player).*" %}

---*I am my sole architect*

I wanted the capacity to load and read more of the runs, and I see a stairway so I follow it down---I got a little carried away with the 1970s hi-fi aesthetics.

---*a non-stop hole in my head*

Zoe loads 75 of the dataset's runs from the dataset, so more ghosts to share and trace: we went trick-or-treating and c-c-came back haunted.

---*a siren on my tail*

Zoe makes noise now: a ratchet tick, four to a turn of the reels, and the speaker on the badge plate turns it off. The [zip](/weblog/assets/preterition-deck.zip), about 270 KB, holds the page, its fonts and their licences, and opens from disk with no server. Under the porch's welcome mat, Reader, as promised: the key.

---*you got to follow me*

It works, and it's loaded. Give the spool a spin, and follow one trace until it lights, goes dark, comes back---or doesn't.
