Hang your head and put on your black beret: the infuriating, impish, and dazzling [Jacques Derrida](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrida) has [passed away](http://www.lemonde.fr/web/article/0,1-0@2-3382,36-382445,0.html).

His was the first really tough and rewarding writing I encountered in graduate school, and like a lot of [other people](http://www.storysouth.com/summer2003/kirby-feature.html#dd), I think I really learned how to read from him. I'm sure there'll be many remembrances and encomia in the next few days; I just think of that passage about "the shadow of the book, the third party between the hands holding the book."
