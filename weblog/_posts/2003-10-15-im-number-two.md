Far less often than, say, the lead singer for Jesus Jones, or that Nascar guy, or that composer, or the soccer player. So I'll take "grading papers" and "fancy words" and whistle all the way to, uh, to the, you know. To the somewhere. [Out there](http://www.google.com/search?q=know+nothing+aesthetic+masturbatory+hooliganism&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8).

Yeah, so maybe it's not all that great an achievement. Modest pleasures.
