The [new issue](http://kairos.technorhetoric.net/11.2/index.html) of *[Kairos](http://kairosnews.org/kairos-issue-11-2-released)* is out, and it's a good one. Of particular interest to some of my librarian peers, for whom I have much respect and whose scholarship I wish my discipline would more fully engage in conversation, might be James Purdy and Joyce Walker's remarkable and compelling essay, ["Digital Breadcrumbs: Case Studies of Online Research."](http://kairos.technorhetoric.net/11.2/binder.html?topoi/purdy-walker/index.htm)

Good stuff from my perspective, and I'm curious to hear what those respected expert peers might say.
