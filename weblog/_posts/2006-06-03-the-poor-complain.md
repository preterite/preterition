In an odd bit of synchronicity, I stumbled across this quoted fine bit of bitter doggerel from 1981 Nobel Laureate in Economics [James Tobin](http://ca.geocities.com/econ_0909meet/tobin-autobio.html) in two different places today. I'm thinking it might make a nice epigraph for my introductory talk at my dissertation defense.

> The poor complain; they always do
> But that's just idle chatter
> Our system brings reward to all
> At least all those who matter.
