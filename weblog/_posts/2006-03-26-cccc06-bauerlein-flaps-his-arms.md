Actually, the full title for this post should be, **"Bauerlein Flaps His Arms and Makes Angry Honking Noises."** As [Clancy points out](http://culturecat.net/node/1054), Emory professor Mark Bauerlein -- in a [recent post](http://www.thevalve.org/go/valve/article/comp_work/) at *The Valve* -- has taken it upon himself to say unkind things all of us who were in Chicago for the past few days.

So: upon what scholarship in rhetoric and composition, Professor Bauerlein, are you basing your critique? Or -- Professor Bauerlein -- have you even *read* any of that scholarship?

Or are you just flapping your arms and making angry honking noises?
