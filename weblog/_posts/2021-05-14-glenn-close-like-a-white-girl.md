I'm sure I'm not the only one from the DC area who had that thought watching the Oscars. I was delighted to see her shout-out to [the city's official music](https://mayor.dc.gov/release/mayor-bowser-signs-bill-designate-go-go-music-official-music-dc), and I wonder how many people who aren't from DC recognize the genre or even remember the song.

https://youtu.be/pDdK3fNUQzQ

The post's title, of course, invokes another hit by E.U.: 1989's "Shake It Like a White Girl," a tongue-in-cheek reference to that era's intersections of race, music, and dance in the nation's capital, on the album that also had "Buck Wild" and a re-release of the collaboration with Salt-N-Pepa, "Shake Your Thang."

https://youtu.be/me6Wt8FWNhU

Go-go's syncopated swing has made other occasional forays into the musical mainstream. Prominent examples include [Kurtis Blow](https://youtu.be/nzP4ogeBN6g), Morris Day and the Time (with ["Skillet,"](https://youtu.be/bX_f1zAoyTw) the addition of rock guitars by Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis seemed inspired in that late 80s/early 90s moment, a la Janet Jackson's "Black Cat" or Cypress Hill, but has not aged well), and even a couple of more recent hits by Beyoncé like "Green Light" and "Crazy in Love": listen for go-go's defining snare and bass drum dotted quarter-and-eighth rhythm during the verse.

https://youtu.be/sFl7OS3WmA0

None of those forays, to my mind, had the force and power of the 7-second snippet of Trouble Funk's "[Pump](https://youtu.be/Sb9SjNEXmpI) [Me](https://youtu.be/592crJUBgGc) [Up](https://youtu.be/WM-BgcLZ0mI)" that Hank Shocklee used as the intro to Public Enemy's galvanizing ["Fight the Power"](https://youtu.be/mmo3HFa2vjg) from *Do the Right Thing*, a song still quickens my pulse every time I hear it. For those unfamiliar with the genre, ["Godfather of Go-Go"](https://youtu.be/Mgjc7iUUnqw) Chuck Brown's [*Go-Go Swing Live*](https://dcist.com/story/16/08/04/the-best-albums-recorded-in-the-dc/) is likely the smoothest introduction, but the high-energy live recordings by [Trouble Funk](https://youtu.be/DcHnjbxottQ) and [Rare Essence](https://vimeo.com/116568916), the latter with its absolutely scorching cover of the Bar-Kays' "Holy Ghost" at 15:54, are more my speed.

And a few weeks before the Oscars aired, I was in DC with extraordinarily fortunate timing, in that I was able to catch the cherry blossoms in full bloom for the first time in decades.
