When they mock-fight, Zeugma always goes to the ground. If my dining room's [the Octagon](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Fighting_Championship), Zeugma's my [Hoyce Gracie](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royce_Gracie). Tink, while more tentative, has [her own moves](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muay_thai), and she's good about cornering and using those front paws in conjunction with her weight: she'll get in close to put Zeugma down, which Zeugma's willing to do and go to work with the hind legs, but then she'll break contact and go for the high ground, usually the arm of the chair, and whap Zeugma around the face from the stand-off distance.
