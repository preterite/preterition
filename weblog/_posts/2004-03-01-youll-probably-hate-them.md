But I gotta say, the best and most inscrutable movie I&#8217;ve seen in a long, long time is about a Japanese family named the Katakuris. I&#8217;ll acknowledge up front that my vocabulary of Japanese film is so embarassingly atrophied (my particular and predictable favorites are *Ran* and *Akira* and *Hana-bi* and *Tampopo* and *Iron Man Tetsuo*) as to show me as a blinkered western bigot, which I don&#8217;t want or mean to be. That said, my best capsule description of [The Happiness of the Katakuris](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0304262/) would be as follows: it&#8217;s *American Psycho* meets *The Sound of Music* on amateur night in Japan. It defies genre and is, at times, so profoundly banal as to be absolutely and embarassingly hilarious. Hyper-violent, sexy, hallucinatory, funny karaoke clichés. Claymation violence. Overacting as the rule. It&#8217;s like the Marx Brothers do a family sitcom [Noh](http://www.iijnet.or.jp/NOH-KYOGEN/english/english.html) [vamp](http://www.nohtv.com/song/pages/read/fanlarge/fan01.htm) on Beckett via Tarantino. Mike sez [check it out](http://www.imdb.com/Reviews/357/35783): [people](http://www.kfccinema.com/reviews/comedy/katakuris/katakuris.html) [love it](http://www.bbc.co.uk/films/2003/04/30/the_happiness_of_the_katakuris_2003_review.shtml) [or](http://seattlepi.nwsource.com/movies/104515_katakuris17q.shtml) [hate it](http://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/HappinessoftheKatakuris-1120270/reviews.php), and you know which camp I&#8217;m in.
