Donald Murray, writer and educator, [passed away on December 30](http://www.boston.com/news/local/articles/2006/12/31/columnist_donald_murray_dies_at_82/). To me and many other writing teachers, [*A Writer Teaches Writing*](http://www.bedfordstmartins.com/bb/curr1.html#383) and "Teach Writing as Process Not Product" are foundational, landmark texts; ones that continue to hold much value.
