Malcolm is with his mom, and Tash waited until he saw her and said goodbye on a video call today. She died tonight.

[![a calico cat resting on a large yellow beanbag](/weblog/assets/tash-a-500.jpg)](/weblog/assets/tash-a-1200.jpg)

She's had her heating pad to sleep on, and she got her meal of tuna this week because I knew she was going, and I feel like she held out to see her boy a final time.

[![a calico cat sitting next to a bouquet of Lego flowers](/weblog/assets/tash-b-500.jpg)](/weblog/assets/tash-b-1200.jpg)

You will be much missed from your place on my lap, dear grumpy old calico, and all shall know that “Tash” was short for “*Tashakor, motherfucker*.” Nelly and Zora and Rascal sniff for your traces in your usual places. May you go in peace to the place of birds and dappled sun, O demander of human food and chin-chucks, O sweet and fearless cat, and may you rest in eternal ease and grace.
