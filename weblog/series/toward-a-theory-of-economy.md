title: "Toward a Theory of Economy"
description: "Every theory of value carries an access regime, a permission to know some things about the agent and not others. Taking up a use is a valuation, and writing's value moves through an economic cycle of production, distribution, use, and re-production. Value is the labor time a society must spend; a party with a stake is a position persons occupy and leave. A ledger reaches only what it can commensurate, a price settles at an instant what labor settles only over time, and a title claims the difference as rent. A contract resets the ratio only where its signers stand, and each taking-up re-produces the stock on which it draws."
