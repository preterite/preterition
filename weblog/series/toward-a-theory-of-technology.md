title: "Toward a Theory of Technology"
description: "Writing is a technology in four senses: artifact, know-how, system of production and system of use. Those four senses are joined by one operation, discretization, which cuts the continuous labor of writing into countable units. Every instrument that reads writing also writes an access clause about what may be known of a writer, and evolving detection technologies yield contestation over control of the record. Technologies substitute capital-intensive processes for labor-intensive ones; the labor saved is stored; and questions of exemption, replacement, or payment are settled outside the machine. Stored labor becomes capital in a relation, placed into use."
