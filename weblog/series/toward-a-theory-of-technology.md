title: "Toward a Theory of Technology"
description: "The series works through a credo: writing is a technology; technologies replace labor with capital; that replacement is an economic act; and the appropriation of writing's value bears investigation. It begins with the clauses writing's instruments write about what may be known of a writer, from the AI detector to the keystroke log, and follows the record those instruments keep to the question of who holds it. It then defines writing as a technology in all four of the word's senses, joined by one operation, discretization. Next it reads technology as the substitution of capital-intensive processes for labor-intensive ones, with the saved labor stored and its incidence decided outside the machine. Last it asks where that stored labor goes, when an archive of writing becomes capital, and whose time it keeps."
