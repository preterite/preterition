---
title: "Toward a Theory of the Subject"
description: "An account of writing as an economy requires a subject. A subject carries assumptions regarding what others may know of its interior. This series names those assumptions an access regime and proposes a subject specified by it: the embodied agnostic subject, materially particular, formed in time it can't recover, addressed to others, with an interior that's real and unreliably available to anyone, the subject included. Marxian, heterodox and information economics and theories of opacity inform specifications of what such a subject needs, which inferences it forbids, and what it makes possible."
