title: "Toward a Theory of the Subject"
description: "Any account of writing as an economy needs a subject, and every subject on offer arrives with a built-in assumption about what a reader can know of its interior. This series names that assumption an access regime and proposes a subject specified by it: the embodied agnostic subject, materially particular, formed in time it can't recover, addressed to others, with an interior that's real and unreliably available to anyone, the subject included. Drawing on theories of opacity and on Marxian, heterodox and information economics, the series works out what such a subject needs, which inferences it forbids, and what it makes possible: systems that contract on observables, self-appropriation of a writer's own archive, and a line between the labor a machine may take and the labor worth protecting. It closes by handing its question to a theory of technology."
