I've kept a mostly academic weblog since June 2003, first at vitia.org and later here. Twenty-odd years of thinking in public: teaching and rhetoric, economics and technology, a run of writing from Afghanistan in 2011 that won Kairos's John Lovas Memorial Award, a long habit of Friday fiction, and the comment threads that engaged and extended the discussion.

It isn't here yet. The archive is moving out of WordPress into the same static form as the rest of this site, and it crosses whole -- published posts, drafts, comments, and the pingback network that tied it to other weblogs. That last part is the slow bit.

Until then, most of it is readable at the Internet Archive. Back soon -- yes, with cats.
