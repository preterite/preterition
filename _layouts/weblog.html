{%- comment -%} This page is out of the search index by declaration: data-pagefind-ignore, not the data-pagefind-body the professional pages carry. A search result should point at an entry, never at a list of entries. The declaration matters because this page asserts no prose of its own. Marking the region as a body and ignoring each card would leave the page out of the index only because nothing indexable was left inside it: a heading, an intro line or a pagination control would put it back with nobody deciding, and with no data-pagefind-meta such a result would show a bare URL where a title belongs. No per-card ignores: once the region itself is ignored they would restate the same rule one level down, and two statements of one rule drift apart. {%- endcomment %}

I've kept a mostly academic weblog since June 2003, first at vitia.org and later here. Twenty-odd years of thinking in public: teaching and rhetoric, economics and technology, a run of writing from Afghanistan in 2011 that won Kairos's John Lovas Memorial Award, a long habit of Friday fiction, and the comment threads that engaged and extended the discussion.

It isn't here yet. The archive is moving out of WordPress into the same static form as the rest of this site, and it crosses whole -- published posts, drafts, comments, and the pingback network that tied it to other weblogs. That last part is the slow bit.

Until then, most of it is readable at the Internet Archive. Back soon -- yes, with cats.
