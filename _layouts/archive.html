--- # _layouts/archive.html -- the weblog's month and category archives, the # pages jekyll-archives writes from site.posts. The weblog page with a list # of entries where the entries themselves would be. # # Two kinds of page take this layout and they differ in one thing, the # heading: a month reads its label off page.date, a category off # page.title, which jekyll-archives sets to the category's own name. The # rest -- chassis, card, count, list -- is one rule for both, because a # reader who follows a date link and a reader who follows a category link # arrive at the same kind of place. # # There is no year archive. Nothing on the site links one: the sidebar's # Archives list is months, and an entry's dateline links its month. The old # /blog/YYYY/ addresses are left to 404 rather than answered by a page built # only to catch them. # # The entries are a plain list and not cards. A reader here is looking for # one entry among many and reads by title and date; stacked cards with leads # would put a screen between one candidate and the next. The lead belongs to # the index, where the reader is browsing rather than hunting. # # Out of the search index by declaration, on the index page's rule: a search # result should point at an entry, never at a list of entries. The same # reasoning carries -- no per-entry ignores, the region itself declared. # # The dateline's visible form and its datetime are post-card.html's, so a # date reads the same on the card it came from and in the list it lands in. --- {%- comment -%} Two assigns before , because the title tag needs the heading: a month archive carries no front matter title of its own, so this layout hands head.html one and the tab reads "July 2004 | Mike Edwards" rather than the site's name alone. The entries are sorted newest first here rather than trusted from the generator, which hands over the posts it gathered and promises no order. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign entries = page.posts | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- if page.type == "category" -%} {%- assign heading = page.title -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign heading = page.date | date: "%B %Y" -%} {%- endif -%} {% include head.html title=heading %}
