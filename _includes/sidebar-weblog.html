{%- comment -%} _includes/sidebar-weblog.html -- the weblog's aside. Sibling to sidebar.html, not a variant of it: the same three zones, with blog navigation where the professional pages carry the calendar. The strip first. Then the blog navigation, inside a landmark of its own, named so it is told apart from the site nav in the header and reached by the layouts' second skip link. Blog nav on the side, site map up top; the sidebar navigates the weblog and never the site. The landmark holds, in this order: Recent Comments a widget list. The five newest across every entry, derived once per build by _plugins/recent_comments.rb from the comment files and the posts they belong to, since a comment file carries no post of its own -- its folder does. Categories a menu. Every category with its count, linking its archive. Archives a menu. Every month holding an entry, with its count, linking its archive. A gap in the writing is a gap in the list. Top Posts a widget list. Ten, hand-picked and hand-ordered in _data/top-posts.yml. Selection as an entry point is not selection as a policy: nothing is removed, and a reader arriving cold at a thousand entries needs a door. The counts are the point: what he wrote about most, and when he wrote the most, read off the shape of the list. A menu is a disclosure the reader opens to select from; a widget list is simply shown. Categories and Archives are the menus and wear details/summary, CSS-only, working with scripting off, on the pattern pages/about.md already uses. Recent Comments and Top Posts are widget lists and wear none. The menus sit between the two lists because a menu wants sidebar space above and below it to select into, so a menu does not sit at the foot of a column. A widget with nothing to show is omitted rather than shipped empty: a disclosure opening onto nothing misleads the reader who accepts the invitation. Before the archive lands, this column may hold the strip, Top Posts, a single month and Listening, and that is the honest state. Listening follows the nav landmark and sits outside it: it is the terminal element of the middle zone, not navigation. Reporter, not navigator: no outbound link, here or anywhere. Its source and selection are _includes/listening.html's, shared with the professional pages' aside and the landing page's sign-off. {%- endcomment -%}
