{%- comment -%} _includes/sidebar-weblog.html -- the weblog's aside. Sibling to sidebar.html, not a variant of it: the three zones are the same and their contents invert. Identity inverts. On the professional pages the header names the author and the zone carries the strip alone; here the header names the weblog and rotates its tagline, so the box does the naming the header no longer does. The middle zone is blog navigation, and it ships with the archive rather than before it. Recent Comments, Recent Posts, Top Posts, Archive by date, Archive by category: until the corpus has migrated and Staticman exists, four of the five would be empty, and a
opening onto nothing actively misleads the reader who accepts the invitation. No footer zone. The dotted box does not render here and the ME bug goes with it: the tagline is promoted to the header, so the box has nothing to hold, and a mark reading "ME" beneath an h1 reading "Preterition" would answer a question the page has already answered differently. So the column is About, strip, Listening -- three elements, all populated, no empty widget anywhere. The About prose is content, read from weblog/index.md's `about:` front matter field and not written here. The card is omitted rather than shipped empty when the field is absent -- an empty identity card is the construction-site reading this column exists to avoid. {%- endcomment -%}
