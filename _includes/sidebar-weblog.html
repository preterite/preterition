Sibling to sidebar.html, not a variant of it: the three zones are the same and their contents invert (RULED 2026-08-21, Michael, site-design-spec.md, The Sidebar's Three Zones). Identity inverts. On the professional pages the header names him and the box supplies the affiliation; here the header names the weblog and rotates its tagline, so the box does the naming and the affiliation has no place. Middle is blog navigation and ships with Workstream 5, not at deploy. Recent Comments, Recent Posts, Top Posts, Archive by date, Archive by category: at deploy the corpus has not migrated and Staticman does not exist, so four of the five would be empty and a
opening onto nothing actively misleads the reader who accepts the invitation. No footer zone. The dotted box does not render here and the ME bug goes with it: the tagline is promoted to the header, so the box has nothing to hold, and a mark reading "ME" beneath an h1 reading "Preterition" would answer a question the page has already answered differently. So the deploy-day column is About, strip, Listening -- three elements, all populated, no empty widget anywhere. CONTRACT: the About prose is content and is authored in the vault, at weblog/index.md's `about:` field.
