{%- comment -%} _includes/comments.html -- the second card on an entry page: the thread, then the invitation and the form. Called from _layouts/post.html with no parameters; it reads the entry's own thread from site.data.welcomments, keyed by the entry's slug, which is the name of the folder the comment files sit in. Order is by date, oldest first, the whole thread in one sequence: a reader arriving at a 2004 entry reads the conversation as it happened. Replies keep their place in time and point at what they answer (see comment.html), so nothing nests and nothing is out of order. Jekyll reads _data/welcomments// as a hash keyed by filename; the `where_exp` below with an always-true test is the idiom that turns it into a list the `sort` filter can take. The card renders whether or not the entry has comments, because the invitation is always true: the form beneath the thread is the way in. When the thread is empty it says so in one line. The form is this site's own markup and posts to the comment service, which checks it for spam and holds it for reading before it becomes a file in this repository and renders here at the next build. The page fetches nothing from the service: no stylesheet, no script, no avatar. The fields are the service's contract, the hidden ones telling it which site and which entry; the reply field is filled by main.js when a reader answers a particular comment, and stays empty otherwise. Nothing here works only with scripting on: the form is a plain POST, and a reader without scripts who wants to answer a particular comment takes its Reply link to the service's own reply page instead. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign thread = site.data.welcomments[page.slug] -%}
